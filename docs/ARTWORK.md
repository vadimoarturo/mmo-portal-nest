# Original fantasy artwork

The application hero uses `apps/web/public/images/fantasy-hero.webp`, an original generated fantasy illustration. It was created with the built-in `imagegen` tool and converted to WebP for delivery. No game screenshots, logos, or character assets were copied into the project.

## Generation prompt

```text

Create a polished premium key visual with the epic, elegant atmosphere of classic Korean high-fantasy MMORPG artwork, inspired by the feeling of Lineage II but featuring entirely original characters and world. On the RIGHT HALF: an elegant adult silver-haired elven battle mage in intricate dark silver armor and flowing ivory fabric, holding a long slender enchanted spear, with a dark armored knight behind her; credible anatomy, beautiful faces, sophisticated detailed armor, not cartoon. Behind them an immense ruined Gothic fortress and distant mountains under moonlit storm clouds, warm gold magical embers and pale blue ethereal light, dramatic cinematic lighting, atmospheric depth, high-end realistic digital concept painting. Keep the LEFT HALF mostly dark atmospheric mountains and negative space for an HTML headline; the upper-left remains deep navy, no detailed subject in front of the headline area. Deep charcoal navy, antique gold, cold silver, subtle crimson accents. Full-bleed artwork, no text, no logos, no watermark, no interface, no buttons, no borders. Design for an evocative MMO landing page, with the characters clearly visible at desktop size and strong silhouette.
```

The desktop and mobile previews are screenshots of the implemented application using synthetic leaderboard data. They are reproduced by `scripts/capture-preview.mjs`.
